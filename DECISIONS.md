# Decisions

One line of reasoning per decision. Newest at the bottom.

- **Build in `./standpoint/`.** The working folder already held other projects.
- **Author identity: `george navarre <georgenavarrecoding@gmail.com>`, set repo-local.** Global `user.name` was unset; this is the identity the owner already uses in other local repositories.
- **pnpm workspaces with packages consumed as TypeScript source.** Vite, Vitest and tsx resolve source directly, so there is no inner build step to drift.
- **zod for the content schema; JSON Schemas generated from it.** One definition validates YAML at compile time and publishes machine-readable schemas.
- **Positions are nested inside their problematic in YAML.** An author sees the full spread of answers to one question at once, which is what fidelity review needs.
- **Tradition ids live in a registry file separate from full entries.** Many authors can reference traditions in parallel before the entries exist.
- **Reserved core position ids.** The trunk and first arc were written before the domain content and needed stable targets.
- **Holding weights: per problematic, resolution = 1 − e^(−evidence mass / 3), and the positive evidence shares that resolution.** Several positions can be held at once, unresolved mass stays explicit, and one strong answer counts as a first guess, not a certainty.
- **Hold threshold 0.25.** A single clear answer registers; a weak lean does not.
- **Probes and follow-ups do not count toward the mode's decision budget.** They are sub-questions of one decision, and counting them would cut a Sketch to six scenes.
- **Sketch enters one arc's escalation standalone, using the node's `recap`.** A full arc would not fit in about 12 decisions, but the crisis choice is the most predictive datum and must be collected in every mode.
- **Survey and Excavation follow a slot template (practice, arc, pressure, arc, trajectory, descent, ...) and fill the rest by expected fog reduction.** This gives each mode a predictable shape while the selection stays adaptive.
- **Challenges are opportunistic, with per-mode quotas (1 / 4 / unlimited), and never interrupt an arc.** Narrative continuity outranks the hunt for contradictions.
- **Undo is an event in the log, not a deletion.** It keeps the session append-only, as the brief requires.
- **Class location is derived from Situation by explicit precedence: ownership outranks the wage relation, and a worker who rents out one flat stays a worker.** The rule is legible and disputable, and it covers the "landlord socialist" case.
- **Reading claims are generated from per-position mechanisms, never from tradition labels.** This enforces "mechanism or silence" and "no taxonomy as verdict" structurally.
- **The labor-aristocracy dispute is stated in full on every core-country drain claim.** This is the brief's "accuracy over line" rule applied where the frame is most contested.
- **Share links carry the deflated session log in the URL fragment and strip Situation by default.** A fragment never reaches any server, and situation data is the most sensitive.
- **Web: Preact plus a tiny signal, hash routing, d3-force for the map.** It is small, static, deploys to GitHub Pages with no server rewrites, and makes no third-party requests.
- **The bundle is fetched from the same origin at runtime instead of being inlined into JS.** This keeps the JS small and lets the dataset be downloaded as a file.
- **MCP server on the current SDK (v2: `@modelcontextprotocol/server`, zod 4).** The SDK docs show v2 as current; v1 is maintenance-only.
- **CLI, MCP and library share one protocol module in core.** All three agent surfaces behave identically.
- **No LLM at runtime anywhere.** The brief requires determinism; same log and seed give the same result.
- **Ontology IRIs under `https://onodofthenorth.github.io/standpoint/ns/`.** They are stable, and the Pages site serves the Turtle and JSON-LD at that path.
- **Arcs planned (24):** night shift (warehouse), the plant (a town losing its factory), IMF conditions, the blockade (first year of a revolution), the pipeline (indigenous land), the surge (far right before an election), rent strike, parish split, land reform, automation, the founder (sell to the workers?), coup, the mine (miners win control; price rises abroad), the clinic (privatization), border town, temple and reservation (caste), uprising after a police killing, war mobilization, microfinance debt, flood reconstruction, platform drivers' strike, campus occupation, a family's law (abortion/marriage in a religious community), and the annotators (AI labor). They span six continents.
- **JSON Schemas for session and result are hand-written (draft 2020-12) and tested with ajv against exports from all three modes.** The result's map sections stay loosely typed so the schema documents the contract without freezing internal analysis shapes.
- **CI runs `compile --strict`, tsc, Vitest, the site build and Playwright in one job; Pages deploys from a separate workflow.** A red content lint blocks merges, and a deploy never waits on e2e flakiness.
- **LICENSE carries AGPL-3.0 for code and LICENSE-CONTENT carries CC BY-SA 4.0 for content, registry and ontology, both copyright the owner.** The split is the brief's, and a header in each file says which paths it covers.
- **The dispute issue template asks for the entry id, the kind of error (description, objection, fact, source, analysis, journey, missing) and a proposed wording.** Description disputes from inside a tradition weigh most, which is the fidelity rule turned into process.

## Phase 2: orientation, frame audit and thinker coverage

- **Every content agent's prompt begins with the orientation (section 0) verbatim, and blind respondents get none of it.** The orientation corrects the author's frame; given to a respondent it would prime the answers being measured.
- **Thinker coverage is a strict lint.** Every leaf tradition has at least 2 thinkers, every problematic has thinkers on at least 3 positions and on its materialist position, and every debate side names a thinker. A materialist position with no person behind it reads as an afterthought; the lint makes that visible and blocks it. Commonsense traditions are exempt (they have no authors by construction).
- **An edge is attached only when the work is verified to argue the position.** Post-1990 works were checked by search; a missing edge was preferred to an invented one, and unverifiable birth years were removed rather than guessed.
- **Thinkers are tagged with the tradition they belonged to, never with an anachronistic one.** Where no registry tradition fits (Leo Strauss, Lynn White Jr., Guénon, Nolte, Auyero's and other historians' declared schools), the person is left out or given the tradition whose frame the cited work argues in, and the choice is recorded in the agent notes.

### Restructured problematics (ids kept wherever the meaning is unchanged, so sessions migrate)

- **`ai.risk` renamed `ai.stake`; question "Who owns AI, whose labor built it, whose work does it cheapen, and where does its rent go?"** The old name and question ("existential risk, tool of capital, or liberation?") made the owners' argument (doom against acceleration) the field. Every `ai.risk.<x>` position became `ai.stake.<x>`; node ids `probe.ai.risk.*` and `debate.ai-risk` were kept so stored sessions still load. New position `ai.stake.core-rent` (monopoly rent of a few US and Chinese firms resting on Southern labelling labor and minerals). `existential` and `differential` now state their drain effect and payers (licensing keeps the rent in a few rich countries). The probe and descent texts that asked for "the main danger", and the descent node staging the release decision as e/acc against x-risk, were rewritten.
- **`technology.nature`: "Whose instrument is technology, and whose interests are built into it?"** The old question was a four-item list. New position `forces-serve-owners` (Lenin on Taylorism, G. A. Cohen), so the Braverman–Noble against Lenin–Cohen debate has both sides. A probe option "Learn to code", mapped to cryptographic escape from the state, was remapped.
- **`growth.ecology`: "Who decides what is produced, and should production grow or shrink?"** The old question listed labels (green growth, ecomodernism, degrowth, ecosocialism, deep ecology) and treated the economy's size as the variable and ownership as a side issue. New positions `socialist-abundance` (Huber, Phillips), `degrowth-communism` (Saito, separated from degrowth), `communities-decide`, and `sovereign-planned-south` (delink, plan for the South's needs; Ajl, Amin, the Patnaiks, Moyo). `ecosocialism` now presupposes participatory, not central, planning (Kovel, Löwy).
- **`population.question`: "Who is counted as too many, and who pays for the answer?"** "Is population the problem?" granted the Malthusian premise. Yes/no labels removed; `plan-it` now has a holder (Song Jian) as well as its critics.
- **`climate.justice`: "Who owes for the heating, and how is the debt settled?"** Every option settled the debt in money while the fuel stayed in the same hands. New positions `expropriate-fossil-capital` (Malm) and `open-the-borders` (Walia, Achiume: the right of displaced people to move). `pay-us-to-leave-it` is now sourced to its holders, not to critics of petro-states.
- **`nature.value`: "Whose is nature, and what is it for?"** The old question was moral and left out ownership. New position `free-gift-of-subsistence` (Mies, Shiva, Merchant, Salleh, Federici), so gender is analyzed in its relation to accumulation rather than listed as an attribute. Three probe options that scored a peripheral people's claim to its own land as blood-and-soil were rewritten so the ecofascist option carries that position's own mark (purity, exclusion).
- **`energy.transition`**: plain now names the mines and whose land the minerals come from. `public-ownership` and `nuclear-abundance` now state `drain: sustains` (minerals and uranium bought at world prices or mined on Indigenous land), consistent with `socialist-abundance`.
- **`automation.ownership`**: new position `whole-chain-workers` (the miners, assemblers and labellers abroad), the answer its own plain asked for.
- **`transhumanism.question`**: new position `bodies-of-the-south` (whose bodies are tested on; Cooper, Vora, Sunder Rajan, TallBear).
- **`accelerationism.question`**: new position `relations-first` (Panzieri, Mao, Bettelheim); the pace probes no longer ask only "how fast". `right-acc` no longer cites Deleuze and Guattari, whose passage is the ancestor of every accelerationism.
- **`ecology.crisis-cause`**: `human-mastery` now states its drain and payers; otherwise unchanged.
- The cold frame audit of ecology and technology and the full per-item fix log are in the agent notes; descent `ecology-technology.02` lost its narrator line calling degrowth against ecomodernism "the main fight".

### Registry and entries added in phase 2

- **`socdem.post-keynesian`** (Robinson, Minsky and successors had no home). **`nationalism.aprismo`** (Haya de la Torre and APRA: anti-imperialism led by a multi-class front, the side Mariátegui answered); full entry with the 1932 Trujillo rising, the 1956 convivencia with the oligarchy, and García's two governments. **`descent.fascist-family`** (10 nodes; each carries a class or anti-colonial option and a plain-conservative one). **`descent.development.11`** (the phone: value capture along the chain) so Emmanuel's and Amin's unequal-exchange positions are reachable. Ba'athism, Kemalism and Peronism entries gained their land, nationalization and union records.
- **Open proposals, not done:** a CEPAL structuralist tradition (Prebisch, Furtado are tagged dependency), a Chinese New Left tradition (Wang Hui), post-left anarchy, an aristocratic-radical home for Nietzsche, a super-exploitation position (Marini), a rename of `world.order` around control of money, finance and technology rents, and frame fixes the political-economy pass proposed for `class.concept`, `intl.sanctions`, `aid.foreign` and `debt.sovereign` (left for the economy and world backward audit).
- **A thinker edge names the work read.** `in` is the primary work; where the attribution rests on a secondary account, the edge carries `via` (author, title, year), shown as "as reported in" on the thinker page, in the result's neighbors, and as `sp:via` in the ontology. The lint fails an edge whose note admits the primary was not read and that has no `via`. First uses: Freda (via Ferraresi 1996), Malliarakis (via the Wikipedia articles, since no scholarly account was found), Zvi Yehuda Kook (via Reuters/Ynetnews 2007 and the Rav Kook Torah adaptation), Magda Portal (via García-Bryce 2015).
- **One-author exception to the two-thinker rule.** A tradition that is one author's work may have one thinker, flagged with `oneAuthor` (the reason) in its entry; thinker-report lists every use. Only use: `technology.r-acc` (Land). Vincent Garton was removed: unconditional accelerationism is a different current from Land's.
- **Aprismo's history includes its reversal.** Haya's thesis (an anti-imperialist state controls foreign capital) and García's (communities without capital block development; transfer their land to large investors, "El síndrome del perro del hortelano", 2007) are both in the entry; García argues `development.path.integration` and `agrarian.question.commercial-farms`, Magda Portal the classical national-popular front.
- **Citation audit (phase 2 thinkers).** A cold sample of 80 of the 1,091 new edges, weighted to post-1990 and non-English works, found 1 error and 1 unverifiable (1.25%; 2.5% with the unverifiable), both on non-materialist positions. Removed: Lomborg's Cool It on `climate.justice.no-inherited-debt`, Ghannouchi's 2016 Foreign Affairs article on `religion.public.separation` (not confirmable), Ellul's La Technique on `accelerationism.question.decelerate` (the book prescribes nothing). Friedman's crisis line re-dated to the 1982 preface. The audit is in `.claude/citation-audit.md`.
